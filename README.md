# Jet-Lag-The-Game-Hide-And-Seek-Manager
So, my friends and me love Jet Lag: The Game, and enjoyed watching their Hide and Seeks, check their channel out

https://www.youtube.com/@jetlagthegame

Of course, we bought their home game, and played hide and seek across Hong Kong
Meanwhile, my friend and me had a Minecraft Transit Railway server with a full built transit system

https://minecrafttransitrailway.com/

"How about playing Hide and Seek in the city?" he asks.

I modified the rules, asked Gemini 3 to write me a program for a game manager, and there here is.

This program is modified for a 15mins hiding period, with all the time halved on the base of Small games.
And also, 1 meter to 2 blocks.

For the hider deck, only cursed doable in Minecraft/online are there, and also include lots of expansion pack curses

For investigation questions, we modified it to the city we built on, Greenfield, it's enormous and beautiful, check it out.

https://www.greenfieldmc.net/

This is just the first version of the game and the manager, feel free to give comments and report bugs. Thank you.
